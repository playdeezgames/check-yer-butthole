Lost and Found

Yer butthole has been holding out on you.

There is a new advancement called Lost and Found. Buy it and every so often a check turns something up. Not often at first, but every level helps.

Whatever you find goes in the new Inventory screen. The Inventory button shows up after yer first find, and finding the same thing again just adds to the pile. Some things turn up far more than others, and one of them is very, very rare.

What are they for? Right now, nothing. Yer just collecting them. What they do is a question for later, and I haven't answered it yet. Not everything in there is going to be good news.

If you already have a game going, it carries over. Yer progress, yer upgrades and yer name are all still there, and Lost and Found starts at level zero, waiting for you to spend some points on it.
